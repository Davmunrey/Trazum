**`trazum bill --agent <name>` bills one coding agent's records, and `--timezone <zone>` cuts days in a zone instead of UTC.** (#826) `--agent claude-code|codex|gemini-cli` (or the name the bill prints, in any case) keeps that agent's records in the statement, the receipt, the side files and `--otlp-out`; a plain log or a provider export belongs to no agent and is left out, said on stderr with the count. An unknown agent is refused by name with the three known ones, and so is a known one with no record among what was read, which exits 1 like every refusal in `bill`. `--timezone Europe/Madrid` cuts `--by day`, `--by week`, `--by month`, the automatic month table, the span line and what a bare `--since`/`--until` day means in that zone, through `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with no new dependency; a day the clocks made 23 or 25 hours long is billed whole. The statement says which calendar it is on. An invalid zone is refused by name before any file is read. `--timezone` is refused, by flag, beside `--otlp-out`, `--markdown-out`, `--html-out`, `--pdf-out` and a `--csv-shape` other than `slice`, which cut their days in UTC, and both flags are refused beside `--git-range`; `--receipt`, `-o` and `--send` combine, because a receipt holds instants and no day. **Without either flag the output is unchanged**, held byte for byte by `bill-agent-timezone.test.js` for `--by day`, `week` and `month`, and the receipt gains no field, so `docs/json-output.md` has nothing to add. Documented in `docs/commands.md`, under `bill`; messages and help in English and Spanish. Planted one at a time and put back, each caught by a named test in that file: the agent filter ignored (in the statement, and in the OTLP file), the zone ignored, an unknown agent accepted, an invalid zone accepted, a zone line printed with no flag, a bare `--until` day ended 24 hours after it began instead of at the next local midnight, `--otlp-out` let through beside `--timezone`, Codex import evidence leaking onto another agent's receipt, a bare `--since` day read in UTC under `--timezone`, and `--agent` accepted beside `--git-range`. The results are recorded on the pull request.
