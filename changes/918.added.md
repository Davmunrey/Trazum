**GOVERNANCE.md, docs/architecture.md and an assurance case say who decides, how the parts fit and why the security requirements are met.** (#918) Governance names the one maintainer, the roles and how a decision is made, and says plainly that the bus factor is one. It sets out what a release rests on if the maintainer can no longer act, and that neither the GitHub successor nor the second npm publisher is in place yet. The architecture page gives the parts, which way they depend, where data goes and what crosses the network. The assurance case ties the threat model, its trust boundaries, the secure design principles and the weaknesses countered into one argument, each step pointing at evidence a test holds.
