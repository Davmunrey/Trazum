**`trazum task` and `trazum filter` speak the reader's language.** (#871) They were run before the language was read, so `--locale` broke them (`trazum task --help --locale es` printed an error) and `LANG` did nothing. Both now take `--locale` and the environment like every other command, and their help and errors, and the errors of the task analysis they run, are in Spanish when the reader asks for it.
