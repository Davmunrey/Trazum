**`bill` says what a Bedrock or Vertex model id is when it leaves the id unpriced.** `us.anthropic.claude-sonnet-4-6`, `anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5-20251001-v1:0` and `claude-sonnet-4-5@20250929` are named with their platform and, when the catalogue knows it, the Claude model inside. They stay unpriced, because each platform sets its own prices, and the hint says how to price them with an overlay. (#858)
