**`trazum gateway --metrics-port <n>` serves what the gateway counted, for Prometheus to scrape, and a Grafana dashboard reads it.** (#824) `GET /metrics` on a second loopback port, off unless given: calls measured, the provider's tokens by kind, their cost at the catalogue's list rates with `trazum_cost_basis="api-equivalent"`, calls with no list price, calls forwarded and not measured by cause, 402s by the reason the refusal body gave, substitutions, free paths and an unreachable provider, all since the process started. The cost is priced by the same function the live budget charges with, `priceGatewayUsage`, taken out of `GatewaySpend.settle` for it. **A second port, not a path on the proxy's**, because `route` is the one reader of a caller's URL there; the scrape port has no upstream, binds to `127.0.0.1` through `listenGateway`, and refuses a rebound page through `refuseHost`. **Counts and names only**: the labels are the provider, the model, `metadata.trazum_label`, a reason and a cause, and no session, prompt or response; nothing is pushed. A model and a label are the caller's text, so past 1,000 distinct pairs a call naming a new one is left out whole and counted in `trazum_gateway_calls_dropped_total`. `docs/grafana/trazum-gateway.json` charts it. Documented in `docs/gateway.md` and `docs/commands.md`; help and messages in English and Spanish. `gateway-metrics.test.js` drives calls through the proxy against a stub provider and reads the scrape; a guard in `security.test.js` holds the port to forwarding nothing, guarding the host first, recording no session, and listening on loopback.
