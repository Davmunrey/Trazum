Label each support message with one of: refund, outage, howto, abuse, other.

Message: My card was charged twice for the same order last Tuesday.
Label: refund

Message: The dashboard has been returning 503 for about twenty minutes.
Label: outage

Message: Where do I change the billing address on my account?
Label: howto

Message: Someone is using my logo on their storefront, please take it down.
Label: abuse

Message: Just wanted to say the new export button is great, thanks.
Label: other

Message: I was billed after cancelling and the site is also down for me.
Label: refund

Answer with the label word only. When two apply, choose the one that costs the
customer money.
